How do I get a key?
  1. Sign up at console.anthropic.com.
  2. Under Billing, add a few dollars of credit. $5 covers roughly ten full interviews.

Typical cost: about $0.40–0.60 for a 10-question interview, and about $0.15 for a 3-question drill. You pay Anthropic directly; this site never sees your bill or stores your key.
